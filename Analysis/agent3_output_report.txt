Schottky emission was rejected solely due to n=5.45, but this high ideality factor (matching the extracted n≈3.2) strongly suggests series resistance is convolving with the true barrier physics—you must implement a Schottky+series-resistance equivalent circuit where Rs is fitted simultaneously with the Schottky parameters before rejecting thermionic emission. The next attempt should be a two-diode model (parallel combination of ideal Schottky diode with n=1 and a trap-recombination diode with n=2, both in series with Rs) to capture multiple current pathways across the Au/ZnO interface that produce the observed n≈3.2 as an effective value. Alternatively, try thermionic field emission which interpolates between pure thermionic emission and tunneling for moderately doped ZnO where the barrier is thin enough for field-assisted escape.
